--- How to read this (lower/higher = better, per column) ---
mean_still_bad_count           : LOWER is better. Channels still >4x median
                                  variance AFTER cleaning = cleaning failed for
                                  that channel. Should be ~0 for a good variant.
mean_known_bad_recall          : HIGHER is better (max 1.0). Fraction of the
                                  manually-identified bad channels (Cz/POz/O2/CP2)
                                  that were automatically caught.
mean_max_variance_ratio_after  : LOWER is better, closer to 1-3 is healthy.
                                  Very high = one channel still dominates.
mean_emg_band_power_after      : LOWER is better = less residual muscle noise
                                  (30-49Hz), BUT check against eeg_band_retention
                                  below -- if that also crashed, the variant is
                                  over-cleaning, not genuinely fixing EMG.
mean_eeg_band_retention         : Should stay reasonably close across variants.
                                  A variant that is MUCH lower than V0_baseline's
                                  value here is destroying real EEG signal, even
                                  if its other numbers look 'better'.
